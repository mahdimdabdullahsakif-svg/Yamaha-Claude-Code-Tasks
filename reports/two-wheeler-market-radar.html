How this runs daily

Sources, every run

  • 7 official sites: Yamaha (ACI Motors), Honda (Bangladesh Honda), Bajaj & Suzuki (Uttara Motors), TVS, Hero (Niloy Motors), Runner, Walton
  • Bikroy.com motorcycle listings (national + Dhaka) for real asking prices

What gets captured

  • Official MSRP per model, diffed against yesterday to catch price moves
  • New SKUs / launch pages appearing since the last run
  • Promo banners, financing offers, festival campaigns on brand sites
  • Bikroy median & range asking price per model, as a street-price proxy

Known limits

  • Public pages only — no dealer-portal or login-gated pricing
  • Bikroy prices are seller asking prices, not verified transactions — directional, not exact
  • New brand-site launches can lag a few days behind press coverage
